# Randomised Coffee Trial (RCT)

A prototype for a platform that matches researchers – any field, any level, honours upward – with one other researcher nearby, once a week, for an in-person coffee. Social connection first, transdisciplinary thinking as the bonus.

**This is a demo, not a product.** Everything is simulated: the people are fictional, ORCID verification is mocked, matching runs locally in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored on a server. All state lives in your own browser’s storage.

## Try it

Open the live link (GitHub Pages), or download `index.html` and open it in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari). No build step, no server, no dependencies – it works offline.

## How it works

- **Double-blind weekly matching.** Each week you privately pick topics you’d love to learn about. Nobody sees anyone’s profile or picks until a match is made at the Monday drop.
- **Mutual matches first.** When two people are each curious about the other’s field, they’re matched as a priority – near-equivalent topics count. Otherwise you get an honestly framed wildcard.
- **No hierarchy.** No titles, no career stages, no institutions required.
- **In person, minimal online.** A small chat exists only to plan the coffee; proposed times carry a venue and duration and a countdown once agreed.
- **Accountability.** Meetups are logged, with optional notes (shareable with your match) and an optional survey feeding an aggregate-only impact dashboard and an anonymised “Sparks” wall.
- **Never re-matched.** Pairs can exchange contact details, revealed only if both opt in.

## Exploring the demo

Use the **Demo controls** button (bottom right) to switch between 36 fictional personas, run the weekly matching, advance weeks, time-travel past an organised meet-up, toggle mobile/desktop layouts and theme, open the matching explainer, or reset the demo.

## Status

Prototype under active iteration. A real version is planned with genuine ORCID OAuth, server-side matching, places-API city search and embeddings-based topic similarity.
